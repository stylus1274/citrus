Project FAQs

What the photos cannot answer.

Can you provide an exact price from project photos?

No. Photos show capability, but pricing depends on the actual structure, access, materials, hauling volume, permits, and final site condition. A site evaluation is required for a reliable estimate.

Do you handle cleanup and debris hauling?

Yes. Debris hauling and site cleanup can be included in the approved project scope so the property is not left with unfinished removal work.

Can you match a project shown on this page?

We can evaluate a similar scope, but every property has different access, utilities, materials, permit requirements, and final-use goals. The estimate will be built around your site.

What types of projects does Citrus handle?

The crew handles residential and commercial demolition, land clearing, site preparation, concrete removal, pool removal, debris hauling, and final grading.

How do I get a project estimate?

Call, text, or complete the estimate form below. The team will review the property, discuss the desired final condition, and build a clear scope around the work required.
