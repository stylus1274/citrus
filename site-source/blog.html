Planning FAQs

Start with the right questions.

Where should I start if I am planning demolition?

Start with a site evaluation. The structure, access, nearby utilities, materials, permit requirements, debris volume, and desired final condition all affect the project scope.

Do demolition projects require permits?

Most structural demolition projects require permits. The exact requirements depend on the property, structure, and local jurisdiction, so they should be confirmed before work begins.

What affects the cost of demolition or clearing?

Major factors include the structure or vegetation being removed, equipment access, materials, hauling volume, disposal, permits, utility coordination, grading, and the condition required at handoff.

Can Citrus handle debris hauling and grading?

Yes. Debris hauling, cleanup, backfill, compaction, and grading can be included when they are part of the approved scope for the property.
